Privacy

Mercury stores selected Gmail metadata, message references, summaries, organization choices, and embeddings while an account is connected. OAuth credentials are encrypted. Full message bodies and raw MIME are processed transiently and are not stored in the application database or job payloads.

When AI processing is enabled, bounded selected text is sent to the configured OpenAI API project for summaries and embeddings. API retention and abuse-monitoring rules still apply; Mercury does not claim zero retention by every processor.

Disconnecting removes the connected mailbox's tokens and derived data. Account deletion also removes the Mercury profile. Managed backups may retain deleted records for their bounded retention period.
