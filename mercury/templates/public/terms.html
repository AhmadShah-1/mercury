Terms

Using Mercury

An organization aid

Mercury is an organization aid, not a system of record. Gmail remains authoritative for your messages, conversations, labels, and unread state.

AI output

AI summaries and possible actions may be incomplete or incorrect and must not be treated as verified financial, legal, or scheduling obligations. A payment or date mentioned in a message is shown as a claim in that message.

What Mercury changes

Bucket moves and “handled” markers change Mercury only. Mercury does not send, draft, delete, archive, or download attachments. Native Gmail labels are written only if both the operator and you enable label sync.

Ending use

You can disconnect Gmail or delete your Mercury account from Settings at any time. See the privacy page for what is removed and when.
