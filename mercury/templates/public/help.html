Help

Getting around Mercury

Short answers to common questions. Gmail remains the place to reply, forward, and open attachments.

What does Mercury change? {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

Bucket moves and handled markers are local to Mercury unless optional label synchronization is explicitly enabled. Mercury never sends, deletes, or archives mail.

Attachments and replies {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

Use Open in Gmail for attachments, replies, and the authoritative conversation. Mercury notes when attachments exist but never downloads them.

“Unread in Gmail” vs. “New in Mercury” {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

Unread in Gmail mirrors Gmail's own unread state. New in Mercury means you haven't opened the conversation in Mercury yet. Reading in Mercury doesn't mark anything read in Gmail.

Why is a conversation in Unsorted? {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

When Mercury isn't confident about a category, it leaves the conversation in Unsorted rather than guessing. Move it yourself, or add an exact-sender rule; manual choices always win over suggestions.

Crates, stars, and Misc {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

A crate gathers related buckets so you can read them as one list. Conversations always stay in their own bucket, so putting a bucket in a crate, or taking it out, never moves or merges anything. Choose Edit next to Crates in the sidebar to drag buckets together, or use a bucket's crate menu.

Star a crate or bucket to pin it in the sidebar. Until you star one, each section shows its three largest. Mercury files its own small suggested buckets into Misc and moves them out as they grow; it never moves a bucket you placed. You can also drag conversations onto a bucket in the sidebar to move them.

A summary says “may be outdated” {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

New messages arrived after the summary was written. The original text in the reader is always current; the summary refreshes in the background.

Summaries are paused or off {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

Off means you chose not to send text to an AI provider, or the operator disabled AI. Paused means the AI budget for the period was reached. Reading and organizing keep working either way.

Connection problems {{ icon("chevron-down", "icon-sm") }}

If authorization expires, reconnect from Settings. Mercury will not silently use a synthetic provider for a real account. If Gmail is temporarily unavailable, try again shortly — saved summaries stay visible but are never presented as the original.
