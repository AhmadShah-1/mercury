How it works

From noise to a quiet overview in three steps

No setup of filters, no new inbox to learn. Mercury works alongside Gmail.

  1. 1

    Connect with a clear disclosure

    Before anything is connected, Mercury shows what it reads, what it keeps, and which AI provider — if you allow it — receives selected text.

  2. 2

    A bounded, visible index

    Mercury indexes up to {{ "{:,}".format(config.INDEX_MAX_THREADS) }} recently active conversations from the last {{ (config.INDEX_LOOKBACK_DAYS / 30)|round|int }} months and shows measured progress — no surprise full-mailbox scan.

  3. 3

    Review, read, and move on

    Correct a few suggested categories, read summaries and the original text, and open Gmail only when you need to reply or grab an attachment.

Built for trust

Automation that stays out of your way

{{ icon("bucket") }}

Categories you control

Suggested buckets you can rename, gather into crates, star, or archive. A conversation you move stays put, a name you choose is never replaced, and uncertain mail waits in Unsorted.

{{ icon("sparkle") }}

Summaries, clearly labelled

One concise AI summary per conversation and conservative possible-action hints. Mark them handled in Mercury — nothing is ever sent, paid, or scheduled for you.

{{ icon("book") }}

Read without leaving

Original message text opens inside Mercury as safe, plain text, newest first. Open in Gmail is always one click away for replies and attachments.

{{ icon("shield") }}

Gmail stays authoritative

No sending, deleting, or archiving. Opening a message in Mercury doesn't change Gmail's unread state, and native label sync is off unless you turn it on.

Our privacy promise

Honest about what's kept

Mercury keeps the information needed to organize your inbox. Full message bodies are fetched when needed rather than retained in Mercury's database. AI processing and provider retention are explained before connection.

{{ icon("database", "icon-sm") }} Kept while connected

  • Selected metadata: senders, subjects, dates, labels
  • Summaries, possible actions, and embeddings
  • Your buckets, moves, and sender rules
  • Google authorization, encrypted

{{ icon("eye-off", "icon-sm") }} Not stored by Mercury

  • Full message bodies or raw email
  • Attachments — they stay in Gmail
  • Email content in your browser's storage
  • Prompts sent to the AI provider

{{ icon("unplug", "icon-sm") }} When you leave

  • Disconnect stops processing and removes derived mailbox data
  • Account deletion also removes your profile
  • Backups expire on a bounded schedule

Mercury is not end-to-end encrypted or zero-knowledge, and it doesn't claim Google approval or certification. Read the full privacy details.
