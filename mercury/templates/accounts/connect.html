Before you connect

Here's exactly what Mercury does with Gmail

Mercury organizes a bounded, recent part of your mailbox. Gmail stays the source of truth for messages, labels, and unread state. Nothing is connected until you confirm below.

{{ icon("eye", "icon-sm") }}What Mercury reads

  • {{ icon("check", "icon-sm") }}Up to {{ "{:,}".format(config.INDEX_MAX_THREADS) }} recently active conversations from the last {{ config.INDEX_LOOKBACK_DAYS }} days — not your whole mailbox.
  • {{ icon("check", "icon-sm") }}Message text when you open a conversation, or when preparing a summary.
  • {{ icon("check", "icon-sm") }}Read-only Gmail access{% if not fake %} (gmail.readonly){% endif %}. Label writing is a separate, optional step.

{{ icon("database", "icon-sm") }}What Mercury keeps

  • {{ icon("check", "icon-sm") }}Selected metadata: senders, recipients, subjects, dates, labels, message IDs, and Gmail's short preview.
  • {{ icon("check", "icon-sm") }}Derived data: summaries, possible actions, embeddings, your buckets and rules.
  • {{ icon("lock", "icon-sm") }}Google authorization, encrypted. Full message bodies are fetched when needed rather than retained in Mercury's database.

{{ icon("sparkle", "icon-sm") }}What goes to the AI provider

If you don't allow it, no message text is sent. You still get buckets you create, sender rules, and the reader.

{{ icon("ban", "icon-sm") }}What Mercury never does

  • {{ icon("x", "icon-sm") }}Send, draft, or reply to email.
  • {{ icon("x", "icon-sm") }}Delete, archive, or trash Gmail messages.
  • {{ icon("x", "icon-sm") }}Download or preview attachments.
  • {{ icon("x", "icon-sm") }}Change Gmail's unread state.
